Who We Are

About the Dance Ready Project™

Our Vision

Elevating the standard of care for dancers.

To bridge the gap between dance science and studios — bringing evidence-based practice to every dancer, at every level, in every institution.

Our Mission

Empowering dancers through knowledge and tools.

To empower dancers and organisations with research-based education, screening tools, and conditioning programs that improve performance and reduce injury across the lifespan.

Our Approach

Three arms, one integrated model

01
Education

Workshops, webinars, and consultations equipping dancers and educators with critical information about dancer health.
