Why It Matters

Built on evidence, designed for dancers.

Dancers are athletes — yet rarely treated like one. The Dance Ready Project bridges that gap with tools grounded in sports science and dance medicine.

  • Research-based screening used by studios nationwide
  • Conditioning programs designed specifically for dance movement
  • Education that closes the gap between what dancers need and what they're taught

Trusted by the best in the industry.

From Alvin Ailey to Netflix's Bridgerton to Tony Award-winning Broadway productions — our methods are field-tested at the highest level of dance performance.
