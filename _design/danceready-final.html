Baseline Fitness Assessment

Dance Ready Score™

Like all athletes, dancers benefit from knowing their fitness baseline before beginning a training season.

What is the DRS?

Your performance GPS

The Dance Ready Score acts as a GPS for dancers — pinpointing current fitness level, identifying goals, and providing a clear roadmap to achieve them.

  • Identifies strengths to build upon
  • Highlights vulnerabilities that may lead to injury
  • Delivers real-time insights into performance capacity
  • Reduces risk of injuries and optimises performance
  • Safeguards financial stability through decreased injury costs
"The DRS provides a framework for structured wellness that serves as an adjunct to technique training."

How DRS is different

  • Immediate results & real-time feedback
  • Language dancers and administrators understand
  • Results for both dancers & administrators
  • No licensure required to administer

Process

How it works

01
Assessment

Dancers go through fitness testing and receive their scores. Quick, non-invasive screening with immediate feedback on performance capacity.

02
Workshop

Review your scores and understand the implications for training, performance, and injury risk.

03
Performance Enhancement

Receive a structured framework to address results. Corrective exercise prescriptions tailored to your unique profile.

Who We Are

About the Dance Ready Project™

Our Vision

Elevating the standard of care for dancers.

To bridge the gap between dance science and studios — bringing evidence-based practice to every dancer, at every level, in every institution.

Our Mission

Empowering dancers through knowledge and tools.

To empower dancers and dance organisations with research-based education, screening tools, and conditioning programs that improve performance and reduce injury across the lifespan.

Our Approach

Three arms, one integrated model

01
Education

Through workshops, webinars, studio consultations, and online content, we equip dancers, educators, and healthcare providers with critical information about dancer health, injury risk, and performance readiness.

02
Fitness Assessment

The Dance Ready Score™ is used by studios, companies, and schools across the country to proactively support dancer health and inform training decisions.

03
Dance Conditioning

Customised interventions and conditioning programs that help dancers strengthen weaknesses, enhance performance, and prevent future injuries — grounded in sports science.
